Hadrian's Wall Path with a Pug: Complete Verified Guide

Route: Wallsend → Bowness-on-Solway | Distance: 84 miles / 135 km | Direction: East to West

Best time: Late May, early June, or September (avoid peak heat for brachycephalic breeds)

Pug-Specific Notes

  • Heat is the #1 risk. Start walking by 8am. If temps exceed 20°C, use the AD122 bus to skip exposed sections.
  • Stiles: Many ladder stiles and kissing gates. A pug is small enough to lift over.
  • Livestock: Lead required through all grazing sections (most of central route). If cattle approach aggressively, release dog and move away.
  • Days 3–4 (Whin Sill crags): Steep rocky switchbacks. Carry pug on worst pitches. Consider a dog sling.
  • Forts: Dogs on leads welcome at Housesteads Fort and visitor centre. Assistance dogs only in museums. Vindolanda does NOT admit dogs. Birdoswald allows dogs on leads in grounds.
  • Pack: Collapsible water bowls (×2), extra water, paw balm, waterproof dog coat, short lead + extending lead, dog sling, poo bags, cooling bandana, dog first-aid kit, towel.

Transport

AD122 Hadrian's Wall Country Bus — Dogs allowed free of charge (confirmed: visithadrianswall.co.uk FAQ). Runs hourly Hexham–Haltwhistle via Chesters, Housesteads, The Sill, Vindolanda, Roman Army Museum. Your pug safety net.

  • Timetable: gonortheast.co.uk/ad122
  • 1-day, 3-day, or 7-day rover tickets available. Hadrian's Frontier 7-day ticket covers ALL Go North East buses.

Bus 185 — Haltwhistle–Greenhead–Gilsland–Birdoswald. AD122 rover tickets valid.

Tyne Valley Line (Northern Rail) — Newcastle–Corbridge–Hexham–Haydon Bridge–Bardon Mill–Haltwhistle–Brampton–Carlisle. Dogs free.

Bus 93/71 (Stagecoach) — Carlisle–Burgh-by-Sands–Bowness-on-Solway. Infrequent — check timetable.

Luggage transfer: Hadrian's Haul (hadrianshaul.com) / Hadrian's Bags (hadriansbags.co.uk). Check dog policies when booking.

Day 8 — Burgh-by-Sands to Bowness-on-Solway

Getting home: Stagecoach 93/71 bus to Carlisle (infrequent — check timetable). Trains from Carlisle to London (~3.5 hrs), Edinburgh (~1 hr), Manchester (~2 hrs), Newcastle (~1.5 hrs).
